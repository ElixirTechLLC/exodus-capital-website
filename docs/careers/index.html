Who We Are

Exodus Capital is a leading commercial real estate investment sales brokerage specializing in the Tri-State Area. We focus on transitioning client equity into cash-flowing investments, advising both generational property owners and institutional funds. Since our founding in 2022, we have successfully sold over 700,000 square feet of commercial properties, totaling more than $270 million in sales volume.
